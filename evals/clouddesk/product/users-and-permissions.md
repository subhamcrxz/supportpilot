# Users and Permissions

## Owner

Can:
- manage billing
- manage organization settings
- manage users
- change roles
- manage integrations
- delete the organization

## Admin

Can:
- manage users
- manage projects
- manage most organization settings
- manage integrations

Admins cannot transfer ownership without the required owner/verification process.

## Manager

Can:
- manage assigned projects
- manage tasks
- invite users to permitted projects

Managers cannot manage billing or organization ownership.

## Member

Can:
- work on assigned projects
- create/update permitted tasks
- comment and collaborate

Members cannot manage billing or organization-wide security settings.

## Viewer

Can:
- view permitted projects and reports

Viewers cannot modify project data or organization settings.

Permission failures should be distinguished from plan limitations where possible.
