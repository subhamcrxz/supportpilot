# Accounts

## Signup

A user signs up with an email address and must verify the email before completing normal account setup.

## Password reset

A user can request a password reset from the login page. Support must not request or provide a password.

## Email change

Sensitive email/ownership changes require verification. Support must not simply change the account email because someone asks.

## Account deletion

Account deletion is destructive and must be initiated by an authorized owner through the supported process. Enterprise accounts may have contractual retention requirements and should be escalated where applicable.
