# Support Policy

## Support hours

Standard support operates Monday-Friday, 09:00-18:00 UTC, excluding major public holidays.

Enterprise priority support has expanded coverage according to the customer's contract.

## Severity

### P0 — Critical
Complete CloudDesk service outage or a widespread production failure affecting multiple customers with no workaround.

### P1 — High
Major functionality unavailable for an individual organization or a severe production issue without a reasonable workaround.

### P2 — Normal
Normal product issue, question, or incident with a workaround or limited impact.

### P3 — Low
General question, documentation request, feature request, cosmetic issue, or low-impact problem.
