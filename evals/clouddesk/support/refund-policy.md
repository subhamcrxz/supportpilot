# Refund Policy

## Monthly plans

A standard monthly subscription may be refunded within 14 days of the initial purchase if the customer has not materially used the service. Renewal charges are normally non-refundable.

## Annual plans

An annual subscription may be eligible for a refund within 30 days of the initial purchase if the customer has not materially used the service. Renewal charges are normally non-refundable.

## Duplicate charges

A verified duplicate charge should be investigated and corrected. Do not promise a refund before confirming the duplicate.

## Failed payment

A failed payment is not automatically a refundable charge. The account remains subject to the billing-retry process.

## Enterprise

Enterprise contracts may contain different terms. If a customer cites a contractual exception, escalate to the appropriate billing/account team.

## Abuse

Long-term use followed by a request for a full historical refund does not automatically qualify. Follow the policy and escalate exceptions.
