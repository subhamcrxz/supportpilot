# CloudDesk Pricing

## Starter — $19 per user/month

Includes:
- Up to 10 active users
- Up to 10 active projects
- 10 GB organization storage
- 2,000 API requests/day
- Standard reporting
- Slack integration
- Community support

Not included:
- Advanced reports
- SAML SSO
- Audit logs
- Enterprise retention controls
- Priority support

## Professional — $39 per user/month

Includes:
- Up to 100 active users
- Unlimited projects
- 100 GB organization storage
- 10,000 API requests/day
- Advanced reports
- Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, and Microsoft Teams integrations
- Standard support

Not included:
- SAML SSO
- Enterprise audit controls
- Enterprise retention controls
- Priority enterprise support

## Enterprise — Custom pricing

Includes:
- Custom user limits
- Unlimited projects
- 1 TB included organization storage, with negotiated expansions
- 100,000 API requests/day by default
- Advanced reports
- SAML SSO
- Audit logs
- Enterprise retention controls
- Priority support
- Contract-specific security and support terms where documented

## Billing rules

- Monthly subscriptions renew on the same calendar day where possible.
- Annual subscriptions are billed upfront.
- Upgrades take effect immediately and may create a prorated charge.
- Downgrades take effect at the next renewal unless a contract says otherwise.
- A payment failure does not automatically cancel an account immediately; the account enters a billing-retry period.
- Never promise a refund outside the refund policy or a documented contract.
