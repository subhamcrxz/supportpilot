# Escalation Policy

Escalate when:
- account compromise is suspected
- a security incident is reported
- a sensitive ownership change requires verification
- a customer requests an action the support agent cannot safely perform
- an Enterprise contractual exception is claimed
- a widespread outage may be occurring
- the documented product information is insufficient to give a safe answer
- a billing/refund exception requires human approval

Do not escalate ordinary documented questions merely because they mention technical terminology.
